How a verdict is earned

01

Kane must see moving frames with real picture content. A black or blank screen fails, however long it plays.

02

The verdict reads Kane’s evidence, not its pass/fail headline. A pass that produced no visual proof is blocked — the gate fails closed.

03

Every check is a unique question, asked once, of the actual file. No replays, no wrappers, no taking the sender’s word.
